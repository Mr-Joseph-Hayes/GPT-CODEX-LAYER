RASTER 0.1 — WINDOWS AUDIO PLAYTEST

Extract the entire ZIP into a new folder, then open Raster.exe.
Use File > Open (Ctrl O) or drag your own NES/SNES ROM into the window.
No games, ROM downloads, or firmware downloads are included.

Sound: Sound button, Ctrl Shift A, or gamepad Back + Start.
Volume: top slider or Ctrl + / Ctrl -.
Mute/unmute: top button or Ctrl M. Muting remembers the volume.
Fullscreen: F11. Pause: Esc. Save states and controller configuration remain
available from the MesenCE menus.

Sound panel gamepad controls use logical XInput labels:
D-pad up/down: focus; left/right: adjust slider or selection.
A: select; B: close; L1/R1: volume down/up.
Printed button labels may differ on an 8BitDo controller.

Reference is the default. Vanilla and Direct bypass audio presentation
processing, including legacy EQ/reverb/crossfeed. They are audio modes, not
an automatic reset of game cheats, patches, HD packs, or video settings.

Defaults: audio on, volume 80%, 48 kHz, requested 30 ms buffer, VSync on,
15% scanlines. End-to-end audio latency depends on the output device.

System spatial audio: use Direct, then enable your own operating-system
spatial renderer. This build is neither THX-certified nor Dolby-certified
and does not implement their proprietary encoders.

This is the native audio milestone. The new full-screen library/boot flow,
manual browser, achievements, browser emulator, and store builds are not
implemented in this release. Existing MesenCE features remain available.

Settings and saves in this portable package stay beside the executable.
Keep this folder separate from an existing Mesen installation.
